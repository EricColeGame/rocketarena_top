## Game Mode Objectives and Match Strategies

Rocket Arena diverges from generic deathmatch games by putting heavy emphasis on game-mode objectives. Each mode features distinct rules for winning, and several trophies require earning specific scores or fulfilling win conditions.

As outlined in our rocket arena trophy guide, understanding how each mode calculates victory points allows you to actively steer matches toward completing associated trophy requirements.

| Game Mode | Core Objective | Dedicated Trophy Target | Recommended Tactics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Knockout** | Blast opponents out of the arena to reach the KO target | Secure multiple KOs without being knocked out | Control high ground and preserve your dodge |
| **Rocketball** | Carry or throw the ball into the opponent's goal | Score goals and force turnovers | Use movement abilities to bypass defenders |
| **Megarocket** | Capture massive landing rockets by holding the zone | Accumulate extended capture time | Deploy area-denial rockets like Blastbeard's |
| **Treasure Hunt** | Hold the treasure chest and collect scattered coins | Hoard coins across multiple rounds | Prioritize high-mobility heroes like Plink or Rev |

### Knockout Tactics

Knockout serves as the core competitive experience. Your goal is filling an opponent’s Blast Meter until an impact triggers a Megablast, launching them out of the arena bounds. To earn clean-sheet knockout trophies, avoid panic-jumping when your meter is full. Instead, preserve your air dodge to reset momentum and bounce off arena walls.

### Rocketball Strategies

Rocketball functions like an explosive hybrid of basketball and rugby. Holding the ball suppresses your primary rocket attacks, meaning successful scoring runs require either coordinated team escorting or agile mobility heroes. Passing the ball to teammates resets dodge cool-downs and creates offensive angles against camped goal lines.

## Artifact System and Loadout Optimization

Artifacts are passive perks equipped to your hero loadout that alter movement speeds, rocket velocity, blast recovery, and ability cooldowns. Upgrading these artifacts is directly tied to multiple silver and gold trophies, notably those asking you to fully upgrade three distinct artifacts to Rank 3.

To upgrade artifacts efficiently, keep the same loadout active across consecutive matches. Every match played with an artifact equipped contributes toward its individual mastery bar, as detailed in this rocket arena trophy guide.

| Artifact Name | Slot Type | Base Effect | Synergistic Hero Playstyles |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blast Protector** | Defensive (Slot 1) | Reduces Blast Meter gain from incoming hits | Heavy frontliners (Blastbeard, Boone) |
| **Cinder's Match** | Utility (Slot 2) | Increases ground and air movement speed | Ball runners and coin carriers (Plink, Amphora) |
| **Powder Keg** | Offensive (Slot 3) | Increases blast radius and impact impulse | Aggressive crowd-control setups (Jayto, Izell) |
| **Booster Puck** | Utility (Slot 2) | Accelerates jump and rocket-jump altitude | Vertical snipers and evasion specialists (Kayi) |
| **Gem of Respec** | Defensive (Slot 1) | Shortens cooldown on ability recovery | Skill-heavy playstyles relying on escapes |

### Maximizing Artifact Synergy

1. **Pick a Static Trio:** Commit to three high-tier artifacts early on. Switching items mid-way splits your match experience across too many items.
2. **Prioritize Universal Perks:** Upgrade artifacts that function across multiple heroes (like movement boosts or blast reduction) to maintain optimal loadouts while swapping characters for roster trophies.
3. **Combine Defense with Recovery:** Defensive artifacts lower the rate at which your Blast Meter fills, making return recoveries far more forgiving.

### Mastering the Blast Return

When an opponent hits you with high blast damage, your character enters the arena's outer hazard perimeter. Instead of accepting the knockout:

- Do not immediately press jump; let the initial knockback impulse decay.
- Fire a rocket at an angled surface to alter your airborne trajectory.
- Use your hero's unique movement utility (e.g., Jayto's thrusters or Amphora's hydro-dash) toward the arena floor.
- Use your final air dodge to cancel lingering horizontal momentum.

Mastering this sequence will secure several tricky return trophies while drastically boosting your match win rate.
