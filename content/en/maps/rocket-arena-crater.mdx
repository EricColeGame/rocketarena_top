### Boom City
Boom City represents the quintessential urban battlefield. Towering skyscrapers create sheer vertical channels, while suspended walkways provide crucial staging areas for objective modes.

* **High Ground Supremacy:** Characters with superior vertical reach, like Raptora, can rain down rockets from rooftop ledges while remaining outside the effective range of horizontal blasters.
* **Trench Traps:** The lower roads carry continuous hovercar traffic. Knocking high-meter enemies into the roadway guarantees a swift blast-out.

### Megadon Junction
Set in the rugged outback of The Wilds, Megadon Junction is defined by wide sightlines, scattered geological spires, and a central railroad track that periodically hosts the massive Megadon Express train.

* **Long-Range Sniping:** Boone excels here. The open skies allow scoped rocket fire to connect across the entire breadth of the map.
* **The Whistle Warning:** When the train horn blares, immediately contest high ground. Any player caught in the central trench when the Megadon Express passes is instantly eliminated, regardless of Blast Meter percentage.

### Golden Zephyr
The Golden Zephyr is a massive, luxurious airship drifting through Gemstone Jungle. It features tight interior cabins, an exposed main deck, and perilous cargo containers dangling off the sides.

* **Close-Quarters Mayhem:** Heroes with area-of-effect suppression, such as Blastbeard, control the interior corridors with ricochet cannonballs.
* **Flanking the Hull:** Plink can blink underneath exterior walkways to flank squads bunkered around the central mast.

## Advanced Movement and Positioning Strategies for Crater Combat

To excel across any stage, you must master the physics engine that powers movement. Rocket jumping is not just a secondary maneuver—it is the bedrock of high-level rotation.

Mastering rotation routes throughout the **rocket arena crater** environment helps your squad control pick-ups and deny return angles:

* **Rocket Jumping Efficiency:** Firing a rocket directly at your feet or an adjacent wall grants a massive upward impulse. Combining this with a standard jump triples your vertical clearance.
* **Wall Climbing:** Firing rockets repeatedly while facing a flat vertical surface allows you to scale sheer cliff faces in maps like Serrated Heights, escaping bottomless pits without expending your dodge cooldown.
* **Air Dodge Conservation:** Save your air dodge for defensive repositioning. Dodging too early leaves your trajectory completely predictable to enemy snipers.

| Movement Technique | Input Sequence | Blast Meter Cost | Strategic Utility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Rocket Jump** | Jump + Primary Fire (Ground) | None (Self-damage disabled) | Quick elevation gain to claim platforms |
| **Wall Climb Surge** | Jump + Consecutive Wall Blasts | Minimal timing delay | Clambering out of out-of-bounds drop-offs |
| **Momentum Slide** | Sprint + Crouch on Downward Incline | None | Rapid traversal through ramps and roofs |
| **Dodge-Canceled Boost** | Jump Pad + Mid-air Dodge | Consumes Dodge Cooldown | Abrupt horizontal redirection to evade snipers |
| **Stall Float** | Aim Down Sights (Certain Heroes) | Temporary Mobility Loss | Hovering above blind corners for aerial ambush |

Coordinate with your teammates to maintain crossfires across primary jump-pad landing zones. When an enemy launches into the air, their predictable parabolic arc makes them exceptionally easy to hit with coordinated focus fire.
