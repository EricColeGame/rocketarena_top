## Core Competitive Modes in Season 2

Rocket Arena built its identity around non-lethal, high-impact projectile warfare where the blast meter replaces traditional health bars. Rocket Arena Season 2 doubled down on this design philosophy by refining scoring cadence, adjusting overtime rules, and rebalancing capture zones across standard and ranked playlists.

Every mode demands a distinct balance between offense, disruption, and zone denial. The table below outlines the core modes active in the playlist rotation:

| Game Mode | Core Objective | Primary Win Condition | Ideal Team Composition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Knockout** | Fill enemy blast meters and knock opponents out of bounds | First team to reach target KOs (typically 20) | 2 Duelists / Slayers, 1 Support / Disruptor |
| **Rocketball** | Secure the neutral ball and carry or throw it into the enemy goal | Highest score at match time limit or reaching score cap (5 goals) | 1 Ball Runner (High Mobility), 1 Zone Denial, 1 Peeler |
| **Mega Rocket** | Capture zones marked by incoming mega rockets | Capture the required number of zones before the opposing team | 1 Anchor / Area Control, 1 Flanker, 1 Zone Cleanser |
| **Treasure Chase** | Collect coins from the treasure chest and gather scattered coin drops | Accumulate the target score during Chest and Coin Blitz phases | 2 Objective Collectors, 1 Heavy Disruptor |

### Knockout: The Classic Combat Trial

Knockout serves as the ultimate test of pure combat mechanics. Unlike standard elimination games where players respawn in fixed positions, getting knocked out in this mode launches you into the stratosphere before a quick parachute descent back into the fray. 

In Season 2, player experience and patch balance tuned the impact of consecutive blast meters. When an opponent's meter reaches maximum danger, knockback resistance drops significantly. Rather than hunting isolated frags, top squads focus fire on a single target to instantly force a blast out before rotating to peel for vulnerable teammates.

### Rocketball: High-Speed Aerial Scoring

Rocketball transforms the vertical arenas into frantic sports pitches. A single metallic ball spawns in the center of the arena, and both teams must battle for possession to carry or launch it through the opponent's circular goal. 

Holding the ball limits your offensive output, turning the carrier into a priority target. Success in Rocketball relies on proactive passing chains. Throwing the ball forward to a leaping teammate or launching it off curved terrain to bypass defender shields remains the highest-percentage playstyle across ranked leaderboards.

```
Neutral Ball Drop ➔ Midfield Skirmish ➔ Ball Possession ➔ Goal Lane Clear ➔ Toss / Carry Into Goal
```

### Mega Rocket: King of the Hill Reimagined

Mega Rocket introduces shifting territorial warfare. Huge projectiles plummet from the sky into designated impact locations across Crater, establishing circular control zones that both teams contest. Standing inside the zone captures it progressively over time, but any incoming blast knockback interrupts capture progress.

Season 2 refined capture radii and visual tell-times for incoming rockets. Surviving in the zone requires proactive cooldown management: defensive bubble abilities, blast traps, and suppressive vertical fire prevent attackers from establishing clean line-of-sight onto the capture point.

### Treasure Chase: Multi-Phase Wealth Hoarding

Treasure Chase divides each round into two alternating scoring phases:

1. **Chest Holding Phase:** A golden chest spawns, awarding coins to the player holding it for every second kept in possession.
2. **Coin Blitz Phase:** Dozens of loose coins rain down across the entire map, sparking a scramble where raw aerial movement routes decide the point differential.

Balancing when to drop the chest to defend yourself versus when to endure incoming pressure to secure crucial coins makes Treasure Chase the most volatile objective mode in the rotation.

## Advanced Tactical Tips for Ranked Playlists

Climbing the ranked ladders in Rocket Arena Season 2 requires more than sharp projectile aim. The best squads utilize precise resource management, camera positioning, and spatial awareness.

### 1. Master the Rocket Jump and Wall Climb Loops
Never stay grounded. Firing a primary rocket directly beneath your character's feet while jumping propels you higher than a standard jump. Combining rocket jumps with wall climbs resets your aerial float time, allowing you to bypass ground choke points and contest overhead goal angles.

### 2. Time Your Item Pickups
Items spawn on predictable timers across every competitive arena. Recognizing when an item pad is about to refresh can swing an entire round:
* **Speed Boost:** Essential for Rocketball carriers to outrun interceptors.
* **Rocket Magnet:** Absorbs incoming fire, enabling risk-free Mega Rocket captures.
* **Trip Mines:** Plant these around the objective perimeter or directly inside the Rocketball goal mouth.

### 3. Track Enemy Blast Meters Before Committing Cooldowns
Do not waste high-cooldown ultimate abilities or heavy blast attacks on opponents with empty blast meters. Monitor the visual UI tags over your targets. Once an opponent's meter flashes bright red, commit your heaviest projectile or gap-closer to guarantee the elimination.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What was the most significant gameplay change introduced in Rocket Arena Season 2?
Rocket Arena Season 2 introduced the hero Leef, redesigned artifact balance, overhauled playlist matchmaking, and refined zone capture speeds across Mega Rocket and Rocketball to emphasize fast-paced team coordination over stagnant defensive turtling.

### How do you score consistently in Rocketball during competitive matches?
Rather than carrying the ball all the way into the goal rim, utilize pass plays. You can throw the ball forward to an airborne teammate or bounce it off structural surfaces into the goal net, preventing the defending team from targeting you with high-knockback rockets.

### Which hero is the easiest to learn for Knockout mode in Rocket Arena Season 2?
Kayto and Blastbeard are widely considered the most accessible heroes for Knockout. Kayto offers reliable multi-rocket spread and an evasive dodge, while Blastbeard provides broad area-of-effect cannon fire that easily catches dodging opponents.

### Does getting blasted out in Mega Rocket reset your capture progress?
If your entire squad is cleared out of the rocket zone, your capture progress remains intact where you left it, but it will rapidly decay if an opponent steps inside the zone uncontested. Maintaining at least one player near the perimeter is crucial to stalling decay until reinforcements arrive.
