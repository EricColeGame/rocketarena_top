### Essential Combat Flight Drills

1. **The Stair-Step Glide:** Never burn your entire fuel gauge in one continuous blast unless you are recovering from a deep out-of-bounds launch. Tap your thrusters in half-second increments. This "stair-step" motion alters your vertical velocity constantly, throwing off enemy prediction reticles.
2. **The Aerial Feint:** Initiate a forward jump as if committing to a low-altitude attack, then immediately burn thrusters while angling backward. Opponents will frequently waste their primary defensive cooldown or fire where they expected you to land.
3. **Pillar Hugging:** Treat tall architectural pillars and rock spires as your pivot axes. Fly around the curve of structures while ascending to break lines of sight, denying enemy snipers a clear line of fire.
4. **Boundary Bouncing:** If hit off the edge at high knockback, do not immediately panic-burn your jetpack. Wait until the initial knockback impulse slows down, use your air-dodge inward toward the arena, and then activate the **rocket arena jetpack** to climb cleanly back over the ledge.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the rocket arena jetpack affect your dodge cooldown?
Equipping or activating the item does not increase your base dodge cooldown. However, because you remain in the air longer while using thrusters, you can pair it with mid-air recovery artifacts (such as Cenote Drake) to regenerate your dodge faster before touching the ground, enabling multiple dodges during extended flight sequences.

### Can you carry the Rocketball while using the jetpack?
Yes, you can carry the Rocketball while activating thrusters. However, your fuel burn rate increases slightly when carrying the objective ball, limiting your total flight duration. Feathering your thrust is crucial in Rocketball mode to maintain elevation without exhausting your gauge before reaching the opponent's goal.

### What should I do if I run out of jetpack fuel over an open hazard?
If your fuel runs out while suspended over a knockout hazard, immediately use your remaining air-dodge toward the nearest piece of solid ground or wall surface. Many heroes can also use primary weapon recoil or mobility skills (like Jayto's Rocket Leap or Kayi's Grapple) to generate horizontal momentum and grab the arena ledge before falling into the Megablast boundary.
