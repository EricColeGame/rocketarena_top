## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many badges can I equip on my profile banner at once?
You can display up to three distinct badges simultaneously on your player profile banner and totem stand. This lets you combine a character mastery insignia, a competitive ranked crest, and a rare lifetime feat badge to showcase a well-rounded combat record.

### Do seasonal ranked badges reset or disappear after a new season begins?
No, your seasonal ranked badges remain permanently unlocked in your collection. While your active competitive rating resets when a new competitive season launches, any rocket arena badge earned during previous seasons stays in your inventory, allowing you to display legacy achievements indefinitely.

### What is considered the rarest rocket arena badge in the game?
The rarest emblems are generally the animated Master Tier seasonal badges and the "Untouchable" lifetime combat badge. Finishing a full match against skilled human opponents without suffering a single Megablast knockout requires flawless mechanical evasion and positional discipline.

### Can I earn badge progression against AI bots in training matches?
Basic combat statistics and select starter challenges can be advanced in cooperative bot playlists, but high-tier combat trophies, ranked emblems, and specific event challenge badges require participation in live multiplayer matchmaking against real opponents.
